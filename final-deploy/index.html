Everything a personal AI should do

Built on official Android APIs and Google Gemini — no fake actions, no silent behaviour. Every result is verified and reported honestly.

Voice Conversation

Natural Hindi, English and Hinglish — including code-switching mid-sentence. Male and female voices, adjustable speed and pitch, continuous conversation with interruption and “stop speaking”.

Gemini Brain

Google Gemini understands your words, breaks complex requests into ordered steps, picks the right tool, and explains failures instead of pretending success.

Phone Control

Start in 3 steps

1

Install the APK

Download /soumya-ai.apk and install it on Android 10 or newer. Allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser when Android asks.

2

Add your free Gemini API key

In Settings → Gemini, paste your own free Google Gemini API key (from Google AI Studio ↗). It stays on your device and is never hardcoded or shared.

3

Grant permissions as needed

Pick your Soumya, choose a voice and language, then enable only the permissions each feature asks for — microphone for voice, notifications for summaries, accessibility for automation.

🔑 Why your own key? Chat needs a Gemini API key because there is no paid backend and no key is bundled in the app. A free Google AI Studio key is enough for personal use. Without a key, chat stays disabled — the app never fakes replies.
